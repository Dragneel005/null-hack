# Push-up Form Coach

Upload a push-up video (side view works best). The app tracks your body with
MediaPipe pose estimation, counts clean vs. partial reps, estimates joint loads,
and gives coaching feedback. Two demo clips are bundled so you can try it instantly.

Built for NullHacks Helsinki 2026 (physical-AI theme).
Runs on the free CPU tier, so analysing a long video can take a minute.
